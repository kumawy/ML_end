# Read-only TRAIN feature audit

Scope: original seed-42 training subset only, 1,787 rows. Read source `data.py`, `legacy_data.py`, `ann.py`, `catboost_model.py`, `ensemble.py`, runner and historical `EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`. No test prediction/result files, validation rows, new scraping, model fitting, product edits, source CSV changes or changed split. The log already records historical test-informed research, which cannot become independent test evidence.

## Top three concrete next candidates

1. **Expose explicit steering as a small corrected-view feature.** All 1,787 training descriptions have a `КПП` boundary. Before it, 93 have an exact isolated `Правый руль` clause; none have an exact `Левый руль` clause or both. Thus 1,694 are **not stated**, not certified left-hand drive. Nineteen brand/model groups contain both right-explicit and not-stated rows, including CR-V, Alphard, Camry, RAV4 and X5; the field is not wholly redundant with model. The unadjusted TRAIN median price is 3m for right-explicit versus 7m for not-stated, but respective median years 1998 vs 2013 make this heavily confounded, not an effect estimate. It is a plausible original source distinction that could reduce systematic price bias and relative errors; no gain is established without training/validation evaluation.

   Exact seam: define pure `_structured_steering` near `data.py:104`, derive `used['steering']` from the existing `specs` near line 113, add it to `CATEGORICAL` at line 27 and, for the currently important corrected ANN, `CORRECTED_CATEGORICAL` at line 33. Keep legacy feature lists unchanged. A standalone conservative proposal is `steering_proposal.py`. It reads no price/ID/model data, requires the `КПП` boundary, full-matches isolated comma clauses and returns `right_explicit`, `left_explicit`, `not_stated` or `conflict`. Unknown/conflicting future categories retain ordinary OHE unknown behavior. A separate numeric `steering_unstated` is optional; the categorical already encodes it. No negated steering wording occurs in TRAIN; the proposal's negated examples are semantic fixtures only.

2. **Add lexical model-family pooling while retaining raw model identity.** Training has 436 `model` categories: 210 singleton categories and 348 categories represented fewer than five times (the latter cover 571 rows). Plain OHE's infrequent bucket loses within-brand relationships; full view additionally repeats near-identical model information as `brand_model`. Derive one coarser *lexical* family using only actual name syntax: Mercedes-Benz `^([A-Za-z]+)\\s+\\d` gives E/S/C etc.; Lexus the same gives RX/ES/LX; BMW `^(\\d)\\d\\d$` yields the first-digit numeric family. Retain unmatched names verbatim; never infer displacement, drive or specifications. BMW has 110 rows/29 names (47 numeric 5-family), Mercedes 127/48 (54 E, 27 S), Lexus 73/19 (28 RX, 16 ES). These 310 rows offer substantially better sharing than raw rare names alone. Narrow predeclared brand-scoped rules avoid merging ordinary different names such as Land Cruiser and Land Cruiser Prado.

   Exact seam: after normalization and `brand_model` construction at `data.py:142–145`, derive `brand_model_family`; add only to the relevant candidate feature-set categorical list. OHE frequency thresholds remain fitted inside `make_preprocessor` (`data.py:235`). Evaluate separately from steering. Do not fit family dictionaries on all splits or use target averages unless they are genuinely cross-fitted inside training folds.

3. **Give ANN a compact age/mileage interaction basis.** Current numeric view has standardized linear year and `log1p(km)` (`data.py:155`), with 1,438 known mileages and 349 missing. TRAIN year/mileage Spearman correlation is −0.726; year/price +0.692 and mileage/price −0.529. A fixed `age = 2025 - year`, `log_age = log1p(age)` and `log_annual_km = log1p(mileage_km / max(age,1))`, retaining missingness, expose plausible nonlinear age depreciation and usage intensity without extra external information. These are deterministic representations, not certified annual usage because registration dates are absent. Predeclare just one small basis and compare it against the same corrected ANN to avoid another broad validation search. An alternative is train-fitted low-knot splines for age/log-km, but avoid simultaneously expanding both variants.

   Exact seam: feature engineering immediately after `data.py:155`; declare in one new controlled numeric feature set near lines 29–33; reuse train-fitted imputation/scaling in `data.py:249`. Use snapshot year 2025, never present year 2026. Preserve raw low/high mileages. Eight TRAIN cars older than 2010 report under 1,000 km, including 777/888/555 patterns, but these may be seller placeholders or genuine readings: the data does not justify target-driven correction, multiplying mileage or removing rows.

## Literal color and equipment: available, lower priority

1,265 TRAIN rows contain a complete exact color comma clause: white 407, black 295, silver 176, gray 169, blue 71, green 42, red 19, burgundy 18, gold 17, beige 13, light blue 12, brown 11, yellow 6, turquoise 4, purple 3, orange 2. A literal categorical color field is feasible; absence must remain not-stated. Do not parse narrative `Европеец кожа салон черный...` as black exterior; this exact TRAIN example refers to the interior. In this format exterior color appears directly after the КПП clause and optional `с пробегом ... км`, before equipment/narrative. A position-aware parser is safer than scanning every mention. Color has good coverage but less direct pricing rationale than steering/family/usage, and may only proxy model mix. Existing structured equipment clauses also include alloy wheels 642, sunroof 194, velour 170 and leather 140; descriptions are truncated, so omitted equipment is not absence. Treat these as separate low-priority ablations, not a blanket text expansion (the history records the full text feature pass failing to improve reliably).

## Semantic traps and leakage controls

* **Do not add original `drive` verbatim.** TRAIN is 23 `awd` vs 1,764 missing, and several `awd` rows merely say `полный электроп...`, `полный комплектация` or `Полный бойял...`. This strongly suggests source extraction from bare `полный` and supports the current exclusion. Only explicit `полный привод`, `4WD`, `AWD` etc. could become a claim; just four TRAIN descriptions contain such conservative terms. No catalog/brand inference.
* Explicit power exists in only three TRAIN rows; expansion there cannot address most errors. The clean engine field is known in 1,786 rows, so missing-engine work has little remaining coverage.
* Source title/model fields are allowable; free description vectorization needs strong safeguards because narrative can include prices/instalments. Never feed price/price_raw, URLs, ad IDs, future test residuals or target-defined price bands.
* `ANN.train_ann`, runner `ann_experiments` and `ensemble.select_blend` currently select exclusively by validation KZT MAE. That cannot enforce the user's **both** objective. Freeze a bounded candidate plan, evaluate both KZT MAE and within±20% on training OOF / authorized validation, and admit a replacement only if it strictly improves both versus the same current ensemble predictions. Do not use a weighted score that accepts a tradeoff or tune thresholds on test. These feature ideas are hypotheses, not a promise that either metric improves.
* Test-informed historical development already occurred. Maintain that disclosure; no fresh external independent generalization claim is justified by selecting another winner on these same historical rows.
